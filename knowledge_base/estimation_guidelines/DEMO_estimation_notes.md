# DEMONSTRATION document (not an official CPWD/PWD/BIS publication)
Paint estimation: litres = wall area x number of coats / coverage per litre, plus a wastage allowance.
Always use the coverage figure printed on the manufacturer's datasheet for the chosen product.
Tile estimation: tiles = floor area / area of one tile, plus wastage (typically 5-10%) for cuts and breakage.
Structural work (foundations, beams, columns, slabs) must be quantified from engineer-approved drawings.
