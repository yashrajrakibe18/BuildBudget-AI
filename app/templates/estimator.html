How this works

  • Quantities are calculated in Python — the assistant cannot change them.
  • Wastage is your allowance for cuts and breakage.
  • Coverage rates (paint, adhesive) must come from a datasheet; they are never guessed.
  • Structural work (foundations, RCC, load-bearing walls) is deliberately excluded.
  • Rates are never invented: leave the unit rate blank to use a stored quotation, or the item is rejected.
